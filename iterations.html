💡 Key Lessons Learned

  1. 1. Simple filters beat complex ones

    Day-of-week and VIX threshold outperformed momentum, gap, and multi-factor models

  2. 2. The VIX filter is protective, not predictive

    2022 had only 20 trades because VIX stayed elevated — this saved capital during the bear market

  3. 3. There's always a Sharpe vs P&L trade-off

    Wider wings and more trades increase total P&L but reduce risk-adjusted returns

  4. 4. Year-by-year validation is essential

    A strategy that works in aggregate might fail in specific market regimes — check 2022 performance

  5. 5. 97%+ win rate is achievable with proper filtering

    But it comes with trade-offs: fewer opportunities and occasional big losses
