💡 Key Lessons Learned

  1. 1. VIX sweet spot exists (14-18)

    Best risk-adjusted returns in moderate volatility. Too low = no premium, too high = too risky.

  2. 2. Dynamic rules beat static rules

    Adapting delta and wing to VIX level improves Sharpe by 15% over fixed parameters.

  3. 3. Conservative delta in high vol wins

    Aggressive delta (higher premium) in high VIX HURTS — win rate drops from 91% to 81%.

  4. 4. Momentum crashes cluster

    Skipping after 3+ down days provides small but consistent protection.

  5. 5. Simple filters > complex filters

    Day-of-week and VIX threshold outperform gap and complex momentum filters.
